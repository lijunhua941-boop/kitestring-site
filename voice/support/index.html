Kite Voice Support / 技术支持

Kite Voice is a voice keyboard for iPhone. Voice features require an annual subscription or a lifetime purchase. Ordinary typing is free.

Kite Voice 是 iPhone 语音键盘。语音功能需年订阅或永久买断，普通打字免费。

Set up / 设置

  1. Open Kite Voice, review the data-use notice, and choose a purchase option. 已购买可点“恢复购买”。
  2. Add Kite Voice in iPhone Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards. Enable Full Access for communication between the keyboard and app.
  3. Allow microphone access when you start dictation. Without a Mac, speech recognition finishes in the Kite Voice app, then you return to your text field.
  4. For Mac recognition, install Kitestring for Mac, configure dictation, and pair on your own Tailscale network. AI polishing requires your own configured AI account.

Purchases / 购买

US pricing: $5.99 per year, automatically renewing, or $19.99 once for lifetime access. No free trial. Local prices appear before purchase. Both unlock the same voice features.

美国区年费 US$5.99，或 US$19.99 永久买断。两种方案解锁相同语音功能，无免费试用；其他地区以购买页价格为准。

Restore purchases from Kite Voice Pro. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple subscription settings. Buying lifetime does not cancel an existing subscription.

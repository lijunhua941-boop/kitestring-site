Kitepost’s engine runs on your own Mac. The Mac watches your mailboxes, separates what needs an answer from what you only need to know, and drafts replies with your own Claude Code or Codex. The iPhone app is where you read, decide, and confirm: nothing is sent until you approve it.

Your Mail Stays Yours

  • No Kitepost server and no sign-up. The app collects no data and has no analytics, ads, or crash reporting.
  • Mailboxes connect on the Mac to your own provider over standard IMAP and SMTP: Gmail, iCloud, and Yahoo with an app password, Outlook and Hotmail with Microsoft sign-in, QQ and 163 with an authorization code, or any other IMAP mailbox.
  • Passwords stay in your Mac’s Keychain. The iPhone never holds mail passwords or AI credentials.
  • The iPhone talks only to your own Mac, over SSH through your own Tailscale network. Pairing is a QR code shown on the Mac.
  • The copy on the iPhone is encrypted, left out of backups, and kept for up to 30 days. You can limit it, clear it, or turn it off.
  • The AI is your own Claude Code or Codex on your Mac, under your own subscription and their terms; the app asks for your consent before the AI is first used. Kitepost itself never sends your mail to any AI service or to anyone else.

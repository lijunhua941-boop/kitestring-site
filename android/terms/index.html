Billing

Refunds and any applicable withdrawal rights are handled under Google Play’s policies and applicable law. Your statutory consumer rights are not limited by these terms.

Requirements and availability

You need a compatible Android device, a configured Kitestring for Mac companion, and a working Tailscale connection. Coding tools and AI services may require separate accounts and fees. Current Pro status must be verified with Google Play; if it cannot be verified, real connections may be unavailable until verification succeeds. The free demo remains available offline. External services, your Mac and network can affect availability.

安全与隐私

  • 你的会话不经过我们的服务器：数据只在你的 iPhone 与你的 Mac 之间、经你自己的 Tailscale 网络端到端加密传输；无法直连时，Tailscale 可能经它的中继服务器转发加密后的流量，中继看不到内容。App 不收集任何数据。你不需要 Kitestring 账号，但需要你自己的 Tailscale 账号。
  • iPhone 的私钥在 iPhone 上生成，存在钥匙串里，不导出、不同步；Mac 的主机身份在配对时固定，变化即阻断连接。
  • 配对时生成的密钥只能进入受管会话，并且只接受来自 Tailscale 地址的登录；不开放公网端口、不改 sshd 全局配置。
  • 它不是沙箱：会话里的 Claude Code 与 Codex 以你的 macOS 账户权限运行。建议开启 App 的 Face ID 或设备密码锁。

Use Your Mac’s Coding Agents from Your iPhone

Kitestring is an SSH terminal and coding-agent session manager for your own Mac, and it works with Claude Code and Codex. Like Blink Shell and Termius, it’s an SSH client: your iPhone displays the session and sends your input; the programs run on your Mac. The app never downloads or runs code on your iPhone.

What It Does

  • Session List
    Numbered Claude Code, Codex, and terminal sessions. The Mac reports whether each one is “Waiting for your response,” “Done,” or “Running.” Sessions that need your attention appear first.
  • Conversation View
    Claude Code and Codex conversations, rendered natively on your iPhone, with tool calls collapsed into a single line.
  • Native Terminal
    A standard SSH terminal with Esc, Tab, Ctrl, and arrow keys in the toolbar. Your iPhone and your Mac can be attached to the same session at the same time.
  • Remote Files
    Browse, preview, and edit files in the session’s working folder and in folders you authorize. Access to keys, credentials, .env files, and other sensitive files is blocked.
  • Drafts and Voice
    Write or dictate a long prompt in a draft, then insert it into the terminal. You can also send photos and files to your Mac.
  • Model & Reasoning Effort
    Switch a session’s model and reasoning effort from your iPhone.
  • Offline Conversations
    When your Mac is unreachable, read recent conversations cached on your iPhone (read-only and clearly marked as not live).
  • Multiple Macs
    Pair one iPhone with several Macs; each one is kept separate.

Security and Privacy

  • Your sessions don’t pass through our servers. Data travels only between your iPhone and your Mac, end-to-end encrypted over your own Tailscale network. When a direct connection isn’t possible, Tailscale may forward the encrypted traffic through its relay servers, which can’t read it. The app collects no data. You don’t need a Kitestring account. You do need your own Tailscale account.
  • Your iPhone’s private key is created on the iPhone and stored in its keychain; it’s never exported or synced. The Mac’s host identity is pinned when you pair, and if it changes, the connection is blocked.
  • The key created during pairing can only open managed sessions, and only from Tailscale addresses. No public ports are opened, and the global sshd configuration isn’t changed.
  • It isn’t a sandbox: Claude Code and Codex run in your sessions with your macOS account’s permissions. We recommend turning on the app’s Face ID or passcode lock.

Seguridad y privacidad

  • Tus sesiones no pasan por nuestros servidores. Los datos solo viajan entre tu iPhone y tu Mac, cifrados de extremo a extremo por tu propia red de Tailscale. Si no es posible una conexión directa, Tailscale puede reenviar el tráfico cifrado por sus servidores de retransmisión, que no pueden leerlo. La app no recopila datos. No necesitas una cuenta de Kitestring, pero sí tu propia cuenta de Tailscale.
  • La clave privada de tu iPhone se crea en el iPhone y se guarda en su llavero; nunca se exporta ni se sincroniza. La identidad del host del Mac se fija al vincularlo y, si cambia, la conexión se bloquea.
  • La clave creada durante la vinculación solo puede abrir sesiones gestionadas, y solo desde direcciones de Tailscale. No se abren puertos públicos ni se cambia la configuración global de sshd.
  • No es un entorno aislado (sandbox): Claude Code y Codex se ejecutan en tus sesiones con los permisos de tu cuenta de macOS. Te recomendamos activar el bloqueo con Face ID o código de la app.
